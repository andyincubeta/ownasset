DAY CLOSE
Applies to valuations, daily snapshots, asset prices and daily charts. MARKET CLOSE = exchange official close (e.g. 16:00 ET). DAY END = last pre/regular/post-market trade before the UTC time (00:00 = midnight ending the day).
WARNING: Yahoo only has extended-hours bars for ~2 years — older dates fall back to the official close. Bars older than ~35 days are hourly (up to 1h early). The time is fixed UTC (00:00 UTC = 20:00 ET in summer, 19:00 ET in winter). Pick a time after the session ends, otherwise prices shift to the next date. Saving a change refetches prices in the background and rebuilds ALL snapshots.
